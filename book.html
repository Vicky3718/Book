Meet Your Guide

"Healing begins the moment we stop fighting our thoughts and start understanding them."

Dr. Ananya Sharma is a renowned clinical psychologist with over 15 years of clinical practice. Specializing in cognitive behavioral therapy and somatic mindfulness, she has helped thousands of individuals navigate anxiety, emotional burnout, and modern stress.

Through her writing, workshops, and clinical sessions, Dr. Sharma bridges the gap between complex psychological science and compassionate, actionable daily practices. The Calm Mind is the culmination of her years of clinical research and patient success stories.
